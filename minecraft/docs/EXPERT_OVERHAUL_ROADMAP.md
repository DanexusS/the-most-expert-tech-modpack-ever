# Expert Overhaul v1 Roadmap

## Goals

Transform the pack into a deeply integrated expert progression pack:

- nonlinear but guided progression;
- meaningful cross-mod dependencies;
- difficult but fair combat;
- complete bilingual quest documentation;
- improved performance and maintainability.

## Phase 1: Audit and foundation

- inventory mods, scripts, configs and quests;
- identify redundant systems and performance bottlenecks;
- create validation tools;
- document baseline metrics.

## Phase 2: Progression redesign

Target progression layers:

1. Survival and exploration foundation.
2. Primitive engineering.
3. Mechanical automation.
4. Electrical industry.
5. Advanced chemistry and logistics.
6. Digital automation.
7. Dimensional technologies.
8. Endgame singularities.

Create will become a major engineering branch instead of the only starting path.

## Phase 3: Recipe integration

Focus on:

- machines;
- generators;
- storage;
- automation infrastructure;
- high tier equipment.

Recipes will use existing mod identities instead of artificial material inflation.

## Phase 4: Combat overhaul

Combat will be balanced through:

- health;
- damage;
- defenses;
- elite encounters;
- boss progression;
- reward scaling.

## Phase 5: Quest encyclopedia

Every important mod receives:

- beginner guide;
- intermediate automation guide;
- advanced systems guide;
- integration quests;
- Russian and English localization.

## Phase 6: Optimization

Measure and improve:

- startup time;
- RAM usage;
- client rendering;
- server tick performance;
- world generation.

All phases will be committed separately and remain reversible.
